# AUDIENCE ADAPTATION & STAKEHOLDER COMMUNICATION

## Task 5: Strategic Explanation (CEO vs. VP of Engineering)

When communicating analysis to different stakeholders, **the underlying statistical findings do not change, but the framing, level of detail, and call-to-action must align with the stakeholder's domain.**

- **For the CEO (Business Impact & Decision Focus):**
  - *Focus:* Financial return, enterprise risk mitigation, timeline to profitability, and explicit budget requests.
  - *Format:* 1-page summary, non-technical language, zero implementation detail.
  - *Core Message:* "Support delays cost us $2.0M annually. Investing $250K yields $400K in retained revenue in Year 1. We need your approval for $250K by Dec 15."

- **For the VP of Engineering (Implementation & Architecture Focus):**
  - *Focus:* System requirements, routing logic, SLA tracking dashboards, resource allocation, and workflow integration.
  - *Format:* Technical design document / PRD, architectural diagrams, sprint milestones.
  - *Core Message:* "To cut response times from 6h to <2h, we need to build auto-triage logic in Zendesk/Salesforce, implement a high-value customer routing rule for accounts >$10K ARR, and create real-time SLA breach webhooks. We estimate 2 engineering sprints ($50K resource cost) targeting completion by Feb 1."

## Task 6: Audience-Specific Executive Summaries

### Version A: Board of Directors (Strategic Risk & Shareholder Value Focus)
> **Executive Summary: Churn Risk Mitigation**
> Unmitigated customer churn represents our single largest operational threat to EBITDA, draining $2.0M in ARR annually (7% churn rate vs 4% industry benchmark). Slow support velocity is driving defection among our highest-margin accounts. We recommend allocating $250K in capital to expand support capacity and implement VIP priority routing. This capital deployment insulates enterprise recurring revenue, delivers a 2x Year 1 ROI ($400K net revenue recovery), and improves long-term customer LTV/CAC ratios. Budget decision requested by Dec 15.

---

### Version B: Operations Team (Implementation & Process Focus)
> **Executive Summary: Support SLA & Queue Restructuring Plan**
> To address current response delays averaging 6 hours, Operations will roll out a revised workflow targeting a strict <2-hour response SLA starting Jan 1. We are introducing automated CRM-based routing to prioritize incoming tickets from accounts with >$10K annual spend into a dedicated tier-2 queue.
> 
> Two new Support Engineers will join the team by Q2 to rebalance daily ticket loads. Operations lead will review weekly SLA compliance dashboards and manage shift coverage to prevent ticket decay during peak hours. Process documentation and SLA training sessions begin Jan 15.

---

### Version C: Support Team (Work Environment & Staffing Focus)
> **Executive Summary: Team Expansion & Workload Relief**
> We recognize that our 40% year-over-year growth in ticket volume has created significant pressure on everyone. To support you and protect your work-life balance, leadership has approved hiring two additional full-time Support Engineers to share the daily workload.
> 
> Additionally, we are introducing new automated routing tools by February to automatically organize incoming tickets, ensuring high-complexity accounts get priority routing so you aren't overwhelmed by backlogs. Our goal is to make your daily workflow smoother, reduce stress, and give you the time needed to deliver exceptional service without feeling rushed.
